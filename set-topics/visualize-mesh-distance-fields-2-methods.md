**VISUALIZE MESH DISTANCE FIELDS** - 2 methods:
.
Method-1: type this CVAR (console variable, a command) into your UE console: `r.MeshDistanceFieldVisualization 1`
Method-2: go to the viewport toolbar and click `Show > Visualize > Mesh Distance Fields`
