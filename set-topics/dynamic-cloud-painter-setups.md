Six different cloud painter setups are located on this level `/Content/FluidNinjaLive/Levels/Clouds.umap`
- Setups 1-4 are using a small-scale, local simulation, painting canvas exposed at the start location - users can interactively paint on these, while in-game.
- Setups 5-6 are using large scale simulation, painting canvas hidden, users can directly paint on the landscape
- Setup 5 erases clouds, Setup 6 adds clouds

As an example, here comes two step by step guide, on accessing cloud-painter setups:

**SETUP-6:**
1. Press `3` in editor to get the camera to a high-altitude viewpoint
2. Press Play
3. Once in Play (in game), press `6` to load the cloud-painter setup-6
4. try to paint on the terrain surface

**SETUP-1:**
1. Press `1` in editor to get the camera to a low-altitude viewpoint (the start location)
2. Press Play
3. Once in Play (in game), press `1` to load the cloud-painter setup-1
4. try to paint on the nearby canvas
