Live v2.0.1.56 does not contain any function to generate gerstner waves.

Optionally, ninja sim buffers could be composited on top of other systems that support gerstner waves:
- unreal water: https://youtu.be/-OvCw4Y4480?si=xBRzjk4JAOWJGMzB
- oceanology: https://github.com/Sartaq12/WaterInteractionPlugin

Integration is up to the users, the above links are examples of users combining ninja with other systems (with gerstner waves)
